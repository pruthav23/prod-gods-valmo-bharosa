Valmo Bharosa is our plan to cut RTO, the parcels that travel all the way to a customer and come back undelivered. It has three working prototypes. Open any one below.

Each prototype opens in a new tab and runs live in your browser. Nothing to install.

Valmo Pakka

Checks that an order is genuine and the buyer is ready to receive it, before the parcel leaves the warehouse.

valmo-pakka.streamlit.app

Open Valmo Pakka

Valmo Pata

Turns messy Indian addresses into precise DIGIPIN locations, so the delivery agent reaches the right door the first time.

valmo-pata.streamlit.app

Open Valmo Pata

Valmo Wapas Nahi

When a buyer refuses a parcel, finds a nearby buyer to resell it locally instead of shipping it back to the seller.

Open Valmo Wapas Nahi

If a prototype takes a while to load

These apps run on free hosting that sleeps when idle. The first visit can take 30 to 60 seconds to wake up. On Streamlit, click "Yes, get this app back up" if prompted, then wait. Later visits load quickly.
